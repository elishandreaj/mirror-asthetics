# Staff Guide – Mirror Aesthetics Dashboard

Open **yourwebsite/admin** on any phone, tablet or computer and sign in with your own email and password.
Never share your login – ask the admin to create an account for each person.

---

## Every morning
1. **Today** – see today's appointments and how many bookings are waiting for confirmation.
2. Click **Review them** (or Bookings → Status: pending) and open each new booking.
3. Check the client's details (allergies, pregnancy, medications). If it's OK, press **Mark confirmed**.
   - If email is set up, tick "Email the client" – they get a confirmation automatically.
   - If not, choose a template under **Messages** → **Copy message** → paste into SMS / Viber / Messenger.

## During the day
- When a client arrives and is done → **Mark completed**.
- Client didn't come → **Mark no-show**. Client cancelled by phone → **Mark cancelled**.
- **Staff notes** are private (e.g. "prefers Jasmine", "paid ₱500 deposit").
- After a treatment, send the **Post-treatment aftercare** message (Messages → Copy).

## Booking by phone, walk-in or Messenger
**+ New booking** → pick the service(s) → fill in name and mobile number (other fields optional) → choose date & specialist → **Show free times** → tap a time → **Create booking**.
Need to squeeze someone in outside the free times? Tick **Override** and type the time (be careful – this allows double-booking).

## Moving a booking
Open the booking → **Reschedule** section → pick the new date → **Show free times** → tap a time → **Move booking**.
Clients can also reschedule/cancel themselves on the website (Manage booking) up to the cancellation limit (default 24 hours before).

## Calendar
Shows how many appointments each day has, pending ones, and 🚫 blocked days. Tap a day to see its bookings.

## Closing for a holiday or a staff day off
**Block times** → choose the date → whole clinic or one person → whole day or a time range → "Repeat for" for several days → **Add block**.
Blocked times can't be booked online. (Existing bookings on that day are not cancelled – move them first.)

## Before & after photos (Results page)
**Results photos** → title (e.g. "Underarm whitening – 5 sessions") → choose the BEFORE and AFTER photo → tick that the client gave **written consent** → **Upload**.
Use **Hide** to take a photo off the website without deleting it. Tips: same lighting, same angle, no faces unless the client agreed.

## Admin-only pages
- **Treatments & prices** – change prices/durations directly in the table (saves automatically), add new treatments, untick *Active* to hide one, tick *Featured* to show it on the home page.
- **Team** – names, titles, photos; "Takes bookings" controls who clients can choose.
- **Promos · Reviews · FAQ** – the home-page promos, client quotes and FAQ answers.
- **Clinic info & settings** – address, phone, hours, map, social links, all page text, privacy policy, **booking rules** (opening hours, closed days, buffer time, cancellation hours, pause online booking) and the **message templates**.
- **Staff accounts** – add people, reset passwords, disable accounts when someone leaves.
- **Activity log** – who changed what and when.

## Exporting
**Bookings → Export CSV** downloads the bookings in the date range (opens in Excel / Google Sheets).

## Privacy reminders
- Client health information is confidential – don't screenshot or share it outside the clinic.
- If a client asks to have their data deleted, an admin opens the booking → **Delete permanently**.
- Log out on shared computers.
